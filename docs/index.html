    Gaps

    Each gap is a chain of claims, numbered in the order the argument runs. The wording of a step is fixed; a step carrying figures has them recomputed on every pipeline run, so the reasoning stays put while the numbers stay current. Evidence chips link to the paper or the company row behind a claim.

    Gap tree

    Every gap, the taxonomy branches it sits across, and the papers underneath. Node colour is severity, fill is how much research exists there. A gap whose branches are nearly empty is a gap in the literature; one sitting over well-populated branches is a gap in synthesis — the work exists and does not add up. The two need different responses.

    The weekly agent

    Every Monday a GitHub Action queries arXiv and a list of lab and security-research feeds, drops anything off-topic with a mechanical gate, then asks Claude what each surviving paper is about, which branch of the tree it belongs on, whether its provenance meets the inclusion bar, what it overlaps, and what it leaves unresolved. Papers that clear the bar are appended to the inventory; the rest go to a review queue rather than being dropped silently. Overlaps, coverage and the gap figures are then recomputed and committed, which republishes this page.
